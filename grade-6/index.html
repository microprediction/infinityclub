∞ Grade 6

For curious 11 and 12 year olds. Math that is just around the corner from what you do at school. One sheet a night, twenty minutes, and a few things that will surprise you.

1Pick a sheetStart anywhere, follow the first thirty nights, or take one of the three short routes that build a single idea over three nights. The Sharpen sheets make school work feel easy. The Around the corner sheets are new ideas.
2Print itEach sheet is two or three pages. The answers print on their own page at the end, so fold it back or hand it to a grown-up.
3Do the stretchEvery sheet ends with a starred problem. You are not expected to get it. Try for ten minutes, then read the answer and see whether you could have found it.
