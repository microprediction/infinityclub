∞ Infinity Club

Math that is just around the corner from what you do at school. One sheet a night, twenty minutes, and a few things that will genuinely surprise you.

1Pick a sheetStart anywhere. The Sharpen sheets make school work feel easy. The Around the corner sheets are new ideas.
2Print itEach sheet is two or three pages. The answers print on their own page at the end, so fold it back or hand it to a grown-up.
3Do the stretchEvery sheet ends with a starred problem. You are not expected to get it. You are expected to try.
