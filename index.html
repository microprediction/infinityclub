∞ Math Detours

The more you see, the less you have to remember.

School takes the main road. These sheets take the short detours beside it: why a minus times a minus is a plus, how codes keep secrets, what infinity is. One printable sheet a night, ten to fifteen minutes.

1Pick your gradeFinding it easy? Pick the grade above. Each grade has its own page with every sheet, a suggested order for the first thirty nights, and a printable tick-off card.
2Print one sheetTwo or three pages. The answers print on their own page at the end, so fold it back or hand it to a grown-up.
3Play with the demosShort on-screen toys that go with particular sheets live in the demo gallery, shared across every grade.
